What does a systems and network administrator do?

Help keep computers and shared services working, connect devices, and manage who can access information.

Understand the technical words

Server: a computer that provides a service to others. Network: the connections between devices. Monitoring: checking services to detect problems. Hardening: tightening settings to reduce security risks.

How I approach my first weeks

A working method: observe, contribute, then suggest improvements. This is an approach, not a record of tasks already completed.

Days 1 to 7

Observe and understand

Learn about the team, tools, procedures and priorities. Ask questions and take notes before changing anything.

Days 8 to 15

Contribute gradually

Handle support or administration tasks with my mentor, have the result checked and improve the documentation.
