Real incident · useful monitoring

Zabbix turned a warning signal into a network correction

Repeated alerts and events appeared on an Avaya switch while users experienced intermittent IP phone outages. Correlating both observations made it possible to focus on the network device instead of troubleshooting each handset separately.

  1. DetectZabbix exposes losses and anomalies associated with the Avaya switch.
  2. DiagnoseMonitoring events are matched with the phone outages reported by users.
  3. CorrectThe Avaya switch is replaced with an Extreme Networks device, with voice VLAN and LLDP-MED adjustments.
  4. ValidateThe phones recover connectivity and update successfully; service stability and quality improve.

05

Looking back

Limits and improvements

Observed strengths

  • SELinux enforcing and firewall active.
  • Dedicated MariaDB application account.
  • History and trend retention already defined.
  • Real screenshots are published only after operational data has been blurred.

Proposed priorities

  • Enable HTTPS with a trusted internal certificate.
  • Encrypt agent traffic using PSK or certificates.
  • Replace direct root SSH with named accounts and keys.
  • Formalise backups and test a complete restore.
